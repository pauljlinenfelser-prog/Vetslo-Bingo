# Vetslo Bingo — Knowledge Base

Vetslo Bingo is an R&D Labs game for the Alson team. It turns the everyday VETRO errors everyone runs into into a bingo game played in a Teams chat. "Vetslo" (said "Vet-slo") is a playful nickname for VETRO on its slow days.

The spirit of the game: VETRO errors are frustrating, so we laugh at them together instead of sighing alone. It runs on the honor system. Nobody fakes bad VETRO luck, because admitting you got hit is already the penalty.

## Getting a card

1. Open the game.
2. Type your full name and click **Draw my card**.
3. Use the **same name, spelled the same way, every time**. Your card is tied to your name. "Mike" and "Mike S." get different cards.

Every card is different. It has 25 squares in a 5 x 5 grid:

- **24 squares** are real VETRO messages, shown just like they look in VETRO: errors in a pink box with a red ✕ icon, and warnings in a yellow box with a yellow ! icon.
- **The center square** is the free space. It's already marked. The free space art is either the burning Vetslo dumpster fire or the VETRO loading spinner, picked at random for each card.
- The columns are labeled **B I N G O** across the top, and rows are numbered 1 to 5 down the side. Squares can be called out like "N4."

Your card stays the same until you get a bingo. There's no weekly reset. Cards never change on their own.

## How to play

1. Go about your workday in VETRO like normal.
2. When VETRO throws an error that's on your card, **tap that square**.
3. The square gets a red circle, and a **callout image is copied automatically**.
4. Go to the Vetslo Bingo chat and press **Control + V** to paste it.

That's it. One tap, one paste.

### What the callout image shows

- The full message in a VETRO-style box (pink for errors, yellow for warnings)
- "[Your name] just got VETSLO'd"
- Which square it was, how many squares you've marked, and how close you are to a bingo (for example, "2 away from BINGO")
- Your free space art in the corner

### Errors that aren't on your card

Click **Share an error**, pick the error that got you from the list of all errors, and a callout is copied. Paste it in the chat. If that error *is* on your card, it gets marked for you too. If it's not on your card, the callout says "Not even on my card. VETRO is just showing off."

### Unmarking

Tapped the wrong square? Tap it again to unmark it.

## Getting a bingo

A bingo is **five marked squares in a row**: across, down, or diagonal. The free space counts.

When you get one:

1. A **Bingo!** popup appears, and a **bingo image is copied automatically**.
2. Paste it in the Vetslo Bingo chat.
3. The bingo image shows "[Your name] got a BINGO!", your bingo number, the error that finished it, and your whole card with the winning row lined in red.

After closing the popup, your winning card stays on screen. Two buttons appear:

- **Share my bingo** copies the bingo image again
- **Start a new card** draws a fresh card when you're ready

New cards are **only available after a bingo**. You can't redraw for an easier card partway through.

The **Bingos** box under the card counts how many bingos you've gotten.

## How cards are built

Errors are sorted by how often VETRO throws them:

- **Common**: everyday errors
- **Uncommon**: show up now and then
- **Rare**: the legendary ones

Every card gets the same mix: **14 common, 7 uncommon, and 3 rare**. So every card is winnable, with a few long shots. The groups may be adjusted over time based on how the game plays. Changes only affect new cards; cards already in progress never change.

## Other buttons

- **Clear marks**: removes all marks from your current card (asks first).
- **Change name**: switch to a different name, which means a different card.
- **Copy restore code**: copies a text version of your card with a restore code on the last line. Only needed if the game can't save where it's opened (see troubleshooting).

## Troubleshooting

**I pasted and got an old image, not my callout.**
The copy didn't reach your clipboard, so you pasted whatever you copied before. After every tap, a small preview of the callout appears in the bottom-right corner. Right-click it, choose **Copy image**, and paste again. This usually happens when the game is open inside SharePoint or Teams; opening it in a regular browser tab fixes it.

**The game forgot my card or my marks.**
Open it in a regular browser and use the same name. The game saves on the computer and browser you play on, so marks made in one browser don't show up in another. Pick one place to play and stick with it.

**A yellow note says the game can't save.**
The place it's opened from blocks saving. Typing the same name still brings back your card. To bring back your marks too, use the **restore code**: click **Copy restore code** while playing and keep it somewhere (or post it to yourself). Next time, paste it into the "Restore code" box under your name.

**Can I get a new card without a bingo?**
No. New cards unlock after a bingo. That keeps it fair.

**Is anyone checking that errors are real?**
No. It's the honor system. Nobody's faking these.

**Does the game send my information anywhere?**
No. Your name, card, and marks stay in your own browser. Only what you paste in the chat is shared.

## Quick answers for Albert

- **What is Vetslo Bingo?** A bingo game made of real VETRO error messages. Get hit by an error on your card, tap it, paste the callout in the chat. Five in a row wins.
- **How do I start?** Open the game, type your full name, draw your card.
- **How do I share an error?** Tap the square. It copies itself. Control + V in the Vetslo Bingo chat.
- **What if it's not on my card?** Use "Share an error."
- **How do I win?** Five in a row, across, down, or diagonal. Free space counts.
- **What happens after a bingo?** The bingo image copies automatically. Paste it, then start a new card whenever you're ready.
- **Does my card reset?** Only when you start a new card after a bingo.
- **Who made it?** R&D Labs.
- **Can I suggest a new error?** Yes. Post it in the Vetslo Bingo chat (a screenshot helps) and R&D Labs can add it to the game.
- **Why are some squares yellow?** Those are VETRO warnings, not errors. They count the same as any other square.
